# 🌟 Welcome & System Overview

**Pragyan Institute Lalganj** is equipped with a state-of-the-art digital coaching platform designed for maximum ease of use. Whether you are a **student** looking to check exam results and pay monthly tuition, a **parent** reviewing fee receipts, or a **teacher/admin** managing batches and attendance, this guide will walk you through every single feature step by step.

### 💡 Key Design Philosophy
- **Zero Complexity**: Simple, clean interfaces with clear buttons and no confusing technical terms.
- **Fast 6-Digit IDs**: All students are identified by intuitive `YYCCSS` IDs (e.g. `261001` = Year 2026, Class 10, Student #01).
- **1-Tap Online Payments**: Integrated with PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, and BHIM with live balance recalculation.
- **Instant Cloud Sync**: All data stays updated in real time with high-speed offline caching.

### 💳 Online Fee Payment: Pay in Full vs Pay in Partial

Students and parents can pay tuition fees online 24/7 through the integrated gateway:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ⚡ CHOOSE PAYMENT OPTION / भुगतान विकल्प                             │
├───────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┤
│  🟢 Pay in Full (100%)            │  🟡 Pay in Partial                 │
│     Full Due: ₹2,000              │     Custom / 1-Month Fee           │
└───────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┘
  ↳ Quick Amount Chips: [ 1-Month Fee (₹1,000) ] [ 50% Balance ] [ Clear All ]
  ↳ 1-Tap UPI Apps:    [ PhonePe ]  [ Google Pay ]  [ Paytm ]  [ BHIM ]
```

#### How to Pay Online:
1. Click **Click Here to Pay Online** or **Submit Online Payment Proof**.
2. **Choose your payment option**:
   - 🟢 **Pay in Full**: Pays the entire pending balance.
   - 🟡 **Pay in Partial**: Enter any custom amount (e.g. ₹500, ₹1,000) or click the **1-Month Fee (₹1,000)** quick chip.
3. The breakdown table updates in real time showing **Amount Paying Now** and **Remaining Balance After Payment**.
4. Tap your preferred UPI app button (**PhonePe**, **Google Pay**, **Paytm**, or **BHIM**) to launch UPI with payee `chandankr1501998@ybl` and the pre-filled amount.
5. After completing the payment, enter the **12-digit UPI UTR / Transaction Reference ID** in the box.
6. (Optional) Attach a payment screenshot and click **Submit Verification Proof**.
7. Admin verifies the UTR within a few minutes, and your balance is instantly cleared!

### 🔑 Resetting Student Password to Date of Birth (DOB)

If a student has changed their password and forgotten it, an Admin can instantly reset it back to their official Date of Birth (DOB) in two convenient ways:

#### Option A: 1-Click from the Student Directory Table

2. Click the amber **Reset Password to DOB** button (`🔑 Reset to DOB`).
3. Click **OK** in the confirmation popup.
4. The student's password is reset to their Date of Birth (e.g. `2010-05-15`) immediately.

#### Option B: From the Student Management Modal
1. Click **Make Changes** on any student row.
2. Switch to the **Login & Security** tab.
3. View the student's ID and official Date of Birth.
4. Click **Reset Password to DOB (YYYY-MM-DD)**.
5. Confirmation is saved and logged in the immutable audit ledger.

### ➕ Enrolling a New Student (YYCCSS Format)

```
  Automatic ID Formula:
  [ YY = 2-Digit Year (26) ] + [ CC = Class Code (10, 09, 08, 07) ] + [ SS = Sequence (01, 02...) ]
  Example: 261001 (Class 10th Student #1 enrolled in 2026)
```

1. In the Student Directory, click **+ Add New Student**.
2. **Select Class**: (e.g. Class 10th ➔ Auto-generates next sequential ID like `261007`).
3. **Fill in Details**:
   - Full Name (e.g. *Ananya Verma*).
   - Parent / Father's Name.
   - Contact Mobile Number.
   - Monthly Tuition Fee (defaults to ₹1,000).
   - Joining Month / Session.
4. **Upload Profile Photo**: Click the photo upload box to attach a student picture directly to cloud storage.
5. Click **Save & Enroll Student**.

# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### For Students & Parents:
- **Q: What is my Student ID?**
  - **A:** It is a 6-digit number in the format `YYCCSS` (e.g. `261001`). Your teacher will provide this upon admission, or you can log in with your registered mobile number.
- **Q: How do I change my student portal password?**
  - **A:** Log into your Student Dashboard, click the *Student Profile & ID* tab, enter your new password in the *Account Security & Portal Password* section, and click *Update My Password Now*. No OTP or verification is needed!
- **Q: What happens if I forget my password?**
  - **A:** Tell your institute teacher or admin. They can reset your password back to your official Date of Birth (DOB) in 1 click!
- **Q: How do I get my fee payment receipt?**
  - **A:** Go to the *Audited Fee Statement & History* tab in your portal and click the green **Download Receipt** button next to your transaction.
- **Q: Can I pay half the fee this week and the rest later?**
  - **A:** Yes! On the payment page, select **Pay in Partial**, enter the partial amount (e.g. ₹500 or 1-Month Fee), and submit your proof.
- **Q: Is my online payment secure?**
  - **A:** Yes, all payments are processed directly through official NPCI UPI apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, BHIM) to the official institute UPI ID (`chandankr1501998@ybl`).

---

### For Teachers & Admins:
- **Q: How do I reset a student's password if they forget it?**
  - **A:** In the Student Directory, click the amber *Reset to DOB* button next to the student's name, or open the *Login & Security* tab in the Student Management modal and click *Reset Password to DOB*.
- **Q: How do I change an admin password?**
  - **A:** Go to the Admin Settings / Password tab, enter your new password, and click save. It is securely encrypted using BCrypt.
- **Q: Can I access the admin portal from my mobile phone?**
  - **A:** Yes! The portal is 100% mobile-friendly with responsive tables, touch-friendly buttons, and mobile camera photo capture.
