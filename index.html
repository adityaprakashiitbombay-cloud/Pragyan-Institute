Frequently Asked Questions

How do Digital Smartboards enhance learning at Pragyan Institute?
Our modern classrooms are equipped with high-tech interactive Digital Smartboards. Complex Science concepts (Physics & Chemistry) and Mathematics theorems are taught using vivid 3D animations, step-by-step digital problem solving, and visual concept simulations, making learning engaging, visual, and easy to understand.
Who are the teachers and what are their qualifications?
We take pride in our highly qualified and experienced mentors:
  • CHANDAN KUMAR — Science Mentor (M.Sc Physics, B.Ed, D.El.Ed, CTET Qualified, 8+ Years Experience)
  • RAVI RANJAN — Maths Mentor (M.Sc Mathematics, B.Ed, CTET Qualified, 10+ Years Experience)
Both educators have an outstanding track record of delivering 100% board results and top school ranks in Lalganj.
What is the fee structure and payment model?
We follow a 100% transparent and Nominal Monthly Fee model with zero admission fees or hidden annual charges:
  • Class 10th (ACHIEVER Batch): ₹1,000 / month
  • Class 9th (NURTURE Batch): ₹1,000 / month
  • Class 8th (ALPHA Batch): ₹800 / month
  • Junior Batch (JUNIO): ₹700 / month
Monthly tuition includes printed assignment modules, weekly board pattern test series, and full student ERP portal access.

What subjects are covered in Class 8th, 9th, and 10th?
We provide comprehensive coaching for Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), English (Grammar & Literature), and Social Science (History, Civics, Geography, Economics) for both CBSE and Bihar Board.
Are demo classes available for new students?
Yes! We offer 3 Days of Free Demo Classes so students can experience our interactive smartboards, teaching pedagogy, and classroom atmosphere before enrolling.
How are board exam preparations handled for Class 10th?
Our Class 10th batch syllabus is completed by November, followed by rigorous 10-year past paper problem solving, weekly Sunday mock tests, and personalized answer presentation feedback to maximize board exam scores.
