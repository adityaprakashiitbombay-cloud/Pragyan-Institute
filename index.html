Frequently Asked Questions

How do Digital Smartboards enhance learning at Pragyan Institute?
Our modern classrooms are equipped with high-tech interactive Digital Smartboards. Complex Science concepts (Physics & Chemistry) and Mathematics theorems are taught using vivid 3D animations, step-by-step digital problem solving, and visual concept simulations, making learning engaging, visual, and easy to understand.
Who are the teachers and what are their qualifications?
We take pride in our highly qualified and experienced mentors:
  • CHANDAN KUMAR — Science & I.Sc Lead (M.Sc Physics, B.Ed, D.El.Ed, CTET Qualified, 10+ Years Experience)
  • RAVI RANJAN — Mathematics Lead (M.Sc Mathematics, B.Ed, CTET Qualified, 15+ Years Experience)
  • ADITI SINGH — Lead English & Grammar Faculty (M.Com, English & Composition Specialist, 5+ Years Experience)
Our educators have an outstanding track record of delivering 100% board results and top school ranks in Lalganj.
What is the fee structure across all classes and batches?
We follow a 100% transparent and Nominal Monthly Fee model with zero admission fees or hidden charges:
  • Senior Secondary (Class 12th & 11th I.Sc PCM / PCB): ₹1,500 / month
  • High School (Class 10th ACHIEVER & Class 9th NURTURE): ₹1,000 / month
  • Middle School (Class 8th BRIDGE): ₹800 / month
  • Middle Foundation (Class 6th & 7th): ₹700 / month
  • Primary Wing (Class 1st to 5th): ₹500 / month
  • Special English Language Wing: ₹500 (Primary), ₹700 (Middle), ₹1,000 (Senior/High) / month
Scholarship Policy: An exclusive 5% concession applies when paying the full annual session fee in a single lump-sum payment.
What subjects and streams are covered across Class 1st to 12th?
We provide structured academic coaching across all key education stages:
  • Class 11th & 12th (I.Sc): Physics, Chemistry, Higher Mathematics (PCM), Biology (Botany & Zoology for PCB), Board Mastery & JEE/NEET foundational drill.
  • Class 9th & 10th (High School): Complete Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chem, Bio), English Grammar & Literature, and Social Science (History, Civics, Geography, Economics) for CBSE & BSEB.
  • Class 1st to 8th (Middle & Primary): Strong foundation in Mathematics, General Science, English Reading & Writing, and concept building.
  • Special English Language Wing: In-depth Grammar rules, Tenses, Clause transformation, formal letter/essay writing, and spoken English fluency.
Can a student enroll only in the Special English batch?
Yes, absolutely! The Special English Language Wing (mentored by Aditi Singh) is completely open for all students — both currently enrolled Pragyan students and standalone language learners from CBSE, ICSE, and BSEB schools looking to master board writing and English fluency.
What are the class batch timings and shifts?
Batches are scheduled conveniently across morning and afternoon/evening shifts:
  • Class 12th PCM/PCB: Mon – Sat, 6:00 AM – 8:30 AM (Morning)
  • Class 11th PCM/PCB: Mon – Sat, 3:30 PM – 6:00 PM (Evening)
  • Class 10th ACHIEVER: Mon – Sat, 4:00 PM – 6:30 PM (Evening)
  • Class 9th NURTURE: Mon – Sat, 2:30 PM – 4:30 PM (Afternoon)
  • Middle & Primary Batches: Scheduled in comfortable afternoon/evening time slots.
  • Special English Wing: Mon – Sat, 5:30 PM – 6:45 PM (Evening).

Our board batch syllabi are thoroughly completed with ample time for revision, followed by rigorous 10-year past paper problem solving, weekly Sunday mock tests under real exam conditions, and personalized answer presentation feedback to maximize board exam scores.
